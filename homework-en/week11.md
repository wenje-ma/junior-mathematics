> **Definition 5.1.3 (Linear Equation in One Variable)**
> 
> An equation that contains only one unknown, and the degree of the unknown is $1$, is called a linear equation in one variable.

> **Definition 5.1.4 (Properties of Equality 1)**
> 
> Adding (or subtracting) the same number (or expression) to both sides of an equality keeps the result equal. If $a=b$, then $a\pm{c}=b\pm{c}$.

> **Definition 5.1.5 (Properties of Equality 2)**
> 
> Multiplying both sides of an equality by the same number, or dividing by the same number not equal to $0$, keeps the result equal.

> **Definition 5.2.1 (Solving Equations by Combining Like Terms)**
> 
> Combine the terms containing the unknown and combine the constant terms, reducing the equation to the form $ax=b$ ($a\ne0$).

> **Definition 5.2.2 (Transposing Terms)**
> 
> Moving a term from one side of an equality to the other side while changing its sign is called transposing terms. The basis for transposing is property 1 of equality.

> **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**
> 
> The general steps for solving a linear equation in one variable are: clear the denominators, remove the parentheses, transpose terms, combine like terms, and make the coefficient $1$.

> **Definition 5.3.1 (Steps for Solving Word Problems by Setting Up Equations)**
> 
> Let an unknown, set up the equation, solve the equation, check, and give the answer.

## Problem 1

(Problem 5.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 10) Sorting a batch of books takes one person $40\,\text{h}$. It is now planned that some people work for $4\,\text{h}$ first, then $2$ more people join them to work for $8\,\text{h}$, completing the job. Assuming these people all have the same work rate, how many people should be assigned to work first?

### Solution to Problem 1

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**, let $x$ people be assigned to work first.

Each person's hourly rate is $\frac{1}{40}$. The first $4\,\text{h}$ complete $\frac{4x}{40}$, and the next $8\,\text{h}$ complete $\frac{8\left(x+2\right)}{40}$. The total work is $1$:

$$\frac{4x}{40}+\frac{8\left(x+2\right)}{40}=1.$$

i.e. $4x+8\left(x+2\right)=40$, $4x+8x+16=40$, $12x=24$, giving $x=2$. Hence $2$ people should be assigned to work first. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 2

(Problem 5.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 11) Set up an equation to solve: for what value of $x$ are the values of the expressions $\frac{2x-1}{3}$ and $\frac{x+2}{2}$ equal?

### Solution to Problem 2

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**.

Set $\frac{2x-1}{3}=\frac{x+2}{2}$. Multiply both sides by $6$: $2\left(2x-1\right)=3\left(x+2\right)$. Remove parentheses: $4x-2=3x+6$. Transpose: $x=8$. Hence when $x=8$ the two expressions have equal values. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 3

(Problem 5.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 12) For what value of $x$ are the values of $\frac{3x-1}{4}$ and $\frac{5x+7}{6}$ opposites of each other?

### Solution to Problem 3

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**.

Set $\frac{3x-1}{4}+\frac{5x+7}{6}=0$. Multiply both sides by $12$: $3\left(3x-1\right)+2\left(5x+7\right)=0$. Remove parentheses: $9x-3+10x+14=0$. Combine: $19x+11=0$, giving $x=-\frac{11}{19}$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 4

(Problem 5.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 13) A boat travels downstream from dock A to dock B in $2\,\text{h}$; returning upstream from dock B to dock A takes $2.5\,\text{h}$. Given that the current speed is $3\,\text{km/h}$, find the average speed of the boat in still water.

### Solution to Problem 4

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**, let the boat's speed in still water be $v\,\text{km/h}$.

The downstream speed is $v+3$, and the upstream speed is $v-3$. The distance between the two docks is unchanged: $2\left(v+3\right)=2.5\left(v-3\right)$. Remove parentheses: $2v+6=2.5v-7.5$. Transpose: $13.5=0.5v$, giving $v=27$. Hence the boat's average speed in still water is $27\,\text{km/h}$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 5

(Problem 5.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 14) A middle school organizes a spring outing. It was originally planned to rent several $45$-seat buses, but $15$ people would have no seats; if the same number of $60$-seat buses were rented, one bus would be left over and the rest would be exactly full. Find the number of students and the number of $45$-seat buses originally planned.

### Solution to Problem 5

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**, let $x$ be the number of $45$-seat buses originally planned.

The number of students: $45x+15$. Renting $\left(x-1\right)$ $60$-seat buses, exactly full: $60\left(x-1\right)$.

$$45x+15=60\left(x-1\right).$$

Remove parentheses: $45x+15=60x-60$. Transpose: $75=15x$, giving $x=5$. The number of students $=45\times5+15=240$. Hence there are $240$ students, and $5$ $45$-seat buses were originally planned. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 6

(Problem 5.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 15) A swimming pool sells summer membership cards each year from June to August; each card is $80$ yuan, for personal use only. With the card, each admission ticket is $1$ yuan; without the card, each admission ticket is $3$ yuan. Under what circumstances do buying the card and not buying the card cost the same?

### Solution to Problem 6

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**, let $x$ be the number of swims.

Total cost with the card: $80+x$ yuan. Total cost without the card: $3x$ yuan. Set the two equal:

$$80+x=3x.$$

Transpose: $80=2x$, giving $x=40$. Hence when swimming $40$ times, the two ways cost the same. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 7

(Problem 5.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 16) A shop opens and, to attract customers, sells all goods at a 20% discount. Given that a certain pair of leather shoes costs $60$ yuan wholesale and, after the 20% discount, the seller's profit margin is $40\%$, what is the marked price of these shoes?

### Solution to Problem 7

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**, let the marked price be $x$ yuan.

The discounted selling price is $0.8x$ yuan. A $40\%$ profit margin means the profit is $40\%$ of the cost, i.e. profit $=60\times40\%=24$ yuan. Selling price $-$ cost $=$ profit:

$$0.8x-60=24.$$

Transpose: $0.8x=84$, giving $x=105$. Hence the marked price is $105$ yuan. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 8

(Problem 5.2, Extension and Exploration, Problem 17) A train travels at uniform speed; passing through a tunnel $300\,\text{m}$ long takes $20\,\text{s}$. There is a lamp on top of the tunnel shining vertically downward, and the time the light illuminates the train is $10\,\text{s}$. From these data, can you find the length of the train? What is the length of the train?

### Solution to Problem 8

By **Definition 5.2.3 (General Steps for Solving a Linear Equation in One Variable)**, let the train be $x\,\text{m}$ long.

The distance through the tunnel is $\left(300+x\right)\,\text{m}$, and the speed is $\frac{300+x}{20}$. When the light illuminates the train, the train travels a distance equal to its own length $x$, and the speed is $\frac{x}{10}$. The train travels at uniform speed:

$$\frac{300+x}{20}=\frac{x}{10}.$$

Multiply both sides by $20$: $300+x=2x$, giving $x=300$. Hence the train is $300\,\text{m}$ long. $\blacksquare$

Walking $15\,\text{km}$ per hour, he arrives $24\,\text{min}$ early; walking $12\,\text{km}$ per hour, he is $15\,\text{min}$ late. Find the prescribed time in hours and the distance in kilometers.

### Solution to Problem 11

By **Definition 5.3.1 (Steps for Solving Word Problems by Setting Up Equations)**, let the prescribed time be $t$ hours.

$$15\left(t-0.4\right)=12\left(t+0.25\right).$$

Remove parentheses: $15t-6=12t+3$. Transpose: $3t=9$, giving $t=3$. The distance $=15\left(3-0.4\right)=15\times2.6=39\,\text{km}$. Hence the prescribed time is $3$ hours and the distance is $39\,\text{km}$. $\blacksquare$

(1) A household uses $84$ kWh in May and pays $35.52$ yuan; find $a$;

## Problem 15

(Problem 5.3, Comprehensive Application, Problem 7) A dairy factory now has $9\,\text{t}$ of fresh milk. If sold directly on the market, each ton yields a profit of $500$ yuan; if made into yogurt, each ton yields $1200$ yuan; if made into milk tablets, each ton yields $2000$ yuan. The factory's capacity is: making yogurt, it can process $3\,\text{t}$ per day; making milk tablets, it can process $1\,\text{t}$ per day. Limited by personnel, the two processing methods cannot run simultaneously; limited by weather, this batch of milk must all be sold or processed within $4$ days. To this end, the factory has designed two feasible plans:

Plan 1: make as much into milk tablets as possible, and sell the rest as fresh milk directly;

Plan 2: make part into milk tablets and the rest into yogurt, finishing in exactly $4$ days.

Which plan do you think yields the most profit, and why?

### Solution to Problem 15

By **Definition 5.3.1 (Steps for Solving Word Problems by Setting Up Equations)**, calculate the profit of both plans separately.

Plan 1: $4$ days of milk tablets make $4\,\text{t}$, and the remaining $5\,\text{t}$ are sold directly. Profit $=4\times2000+5\times500=8000+2500=10500$ yuan.

Plan 2: let milk tablets take $x$ days and yogurt take $\left(4-x\right)$ days. The amount of milk tablets is $x\,\text{t}$, and yogurt is $3\left(4-x\right)\,\text{t}$. Total $9\,\text{t}$: $x+3\left(4-x\right)=9$. Simplifying: $x+12-3x=9$, $-2x=-3$, giving $x=1.5$. Milk tablets $1.5\,\text{t}$, yogurt $7.5\,\text{t}$. Profit $=1.5\times2000+7.5\times1200=3000+9000=12000$ yuan.

Plan 2 profit $12000>10500$, so Plan 2 yields the most profit. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 19

(Problem 5.3, Extension and Exploration, Problem 11) A company makes a product with a cost of $400$ yuan per piece and a selling price of $510$ yuan. This quarter it sold $m$ pieces. To further expand the market, the company decides to lower the cost while lowering the selling price. After market research, it predicts that next quarter the selling price per piece will drop $4\%$ and sales volume will rise $10\%$. To keep the sales profit unchanged, by how many yuan should the cost per piece be reduced?

### Solution to Problem 19

By **Definition 5.3.1 (Steps for Solving Word Problems by Setting Up Equations)**, let the cost be reduced by $x$ yuan per piece.

This quarter's profit: $\left(510-400\right)m=110m$. Next quarter's selling price: $510\times\left(1-4\%\right)=510\times0.96=489.6$ yuan. Sales volume: $m\left(1+10\%\right)=1.1m$. Next quarter's cost: $\left(400-x\right)$ yuan. Profit unchanged:

$$\left(489.6-400+x\right)\times1.1m=110m.$$

Simplifying: $\left(89.6+x\right)\times1.1=110$. $98.56+1.1x=110$. $1.1x=11.44$. Giving $x=10.4$. Hence the cost per piece should be reduced by $10.4$ yuan. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 20

(Problem 5.3, Extension and Exploration, Problem 12) A boat travels downstream from dock A to dock B, then returns upstream, reaching dock C which lies between A and B; the total navigation is $7\,\text{h}$. Given that the boat's speed in still water is $7.5\,\text{km/h}$, the current speed is $2.5\,\text{km/h}$, and the voyage between docks A and C is $10\,\text{km}$, find the voyage between docks A and B.

### Solution to Problem 20

By **Definition 5.3.1 (Steps for Solving Word Problems by Setting Up Equations)**, let the A-to-B voyage be $x\,\text{km}$.

The downstream speed $=7.5+2.5=10\,\text{km/h}$, and the upstream speed $=7.5-2.5=5\,\text{km/h}$. The B-to-C voyage is $\left(x-10\right)\,\text{km}$. Total time:

$$\frac{x}{10}+\frac{x-10}{5}=7.$$

Multiply both sides by $10$: $x+2\left(x-10\right)=70$. Remove parentheses: $x+2x-20=70$. Combine: $3x=90$, giving $x=30$. Hence the voyage between docks A and B is $30\,\text{km}$. $\blacksquare$
