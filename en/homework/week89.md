> **Definition 18.3.1** (Addition and Subtraction of Fractions with the Same Denominator) <br>To add or subtract fractions with the same denominator, keep the denominator unchanged and add or subtract the numerators, i.e. $\frac{a}{c}\pm\frac{b}{c}=\frac{a\pm b}{c}$.

> **Definition 18.3.2** (Addition and Subtraction of Fractions with Different Denominators) <br>To add or subtract fractions with different denominators, first find a common denominator to convert them into fractions with the same denominator, then add or subtract, i.e. $\frac{a}{b}\pm\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad\pm bc}{bd}$.

### Exercise 1

(Exercise 18.3, Problem 1) Calculate:

(1) $\frac{3}{a}+\frac{1}{a}$;

(2) $\frac{m}{m-n}-\frac{n}{m-n}$;

(3) $\frac{x^2}{x+y}+\frac{y^2+2xy}{x+y}$;

(4) $\frac{a^2}{a-1}-a-1$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 18.3.1** (Addition and Subtraction of Fractions with the Same Denominator).

(1) $\frac{3}{a}+\frac{1}{a}=\frac{4}{a}$.

(2) $\frac{m}{m-n}-\frac{n}{m-n}=\frac{m-n}{m-n}=1$.

(3) $\frac{x^2}{x+y}+\frac{y^2+2xy}{x+y}=\frac{x^2+2xy+y^2}{x+y}=\frac{(x+y)^2}{x+y}=x+y$.

(4) $\frac{a^2}{a-1}-a-1=\frac{a^2-(a+1)(a-1)}{a-1}=\frac{a^2-(a^2-1)}{a-1}=\frac{1}{a-1}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 2

(Exercise 18.3, Problem 1) Calculate:

(1) $\frac{1}{2 c^2 d}+\frac{1}{3 c d^2}$;

(2) $\frac{1}{p-q}+\frac{1}{p+q}$;

(3) $\frac{a}{a^2-4}-\frac{1}{a+2}$;

(4) $\frac{a}{a-b}+\frac{b^2}{ab-a^2}$.

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 18.3.2** (Addition and Subtraction of Fractions with Different Denominators).

(1) Common denominator $6c^2 d^2$: $\frac{1}{2c^2 d}+\frac{1}{3cd^2}=\frac{3d+2c}{6c^2 d^2}$.

(2) Common denominator $(p-q)(p+q)$: $\frac{(p+q)+(p-q)}{(p-q)(p+q)}=\frac{2p}{p^2-q^2}$.

(3) Common denominator $(a+2)(a-2)$: $\frac{a}{(a+2)(a-2)}-\frac{a-2}{(a+2)(a-2)}=\frac{a-(a-2)}{(a+2)(a-2)}=\frac{2}{a^2-4}$.

(4) $\frac{a}{a-b}+\frac{b^2}{a(b-a)}=\frac{a}{a-b}-\frac{b^2}{a(a-b)}=\frac{a^2-b^2}{a(a-b)}=\frac{a+b}{a}$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 1

(Exercise 18.3, Review and Consolidation, Problem 1) Calculate:

(1) $\frac{3}{a}+\frac{a-15}{5 a}$;

(2) $\frac{2 y}{3 x^2 y^2}+\frac{5 x}{3 x^2 y^2}$;

(3) $\frac{a+1}{a-1}-\frac{a}{a-1}$;

(4) $\frac{x^2}{x-2}-\frac{4 x}{x-2}+\frac{4}{x-2}$.

### Solution to Problem 1

By **Definition 18.3.1**.

(1) $\frac{15}{5a}+\frac{a-15}{5a}=\frac{a}{5a}=\frac15$.

(2) $\frac{2y+5x}{3x^2 y^2}$.

(3) $\frac{a+1-a}{a-1}=\frac{1}{a-1}$.

(4) $\frac{x^2-4x+4}{x-2}=\frac{(x-2)^2}{x-2}=x-2$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 2

(Exercise 18.3, Review and Consolidation, Problem 2) Calculate:

(1) $\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{x+1}$;

(2) $\frac{1}{a+2}-\frac{1}{a-2}$;

(3) $\frac{1}{x+y}+\frac{1}{x-y}$;

(4) $\frac{x}{x+1}-\frac{2}{x^2-1}$.

### Solution to Problem 2

By **Definition 18.3.2**.

(1) $\frac{(x+1)-x}{x(x+1)}=\frac{1}{x(x+1)}$.

(2) $\frac{(a-2)-(a+2)}{(a+2)(a-2)}=\frac{-4}{a^2-4}=-\frac{4}{a^2-4}$.

(3) $\frac{(x-y)+(x+y)}{(x+y)(x-y)}=\frac{2x}{x^2-y^2}$.

(4) $\frac{x}{x+1}-\frac{2}{(x+1)(x-1)}=\frac{x(x-1)-2}{(x+1)(x-1)}=\frac{x^2-x-2}{(x+1)(x-1)}=\frac{(x-2)(x+1)}{(x+1)(x-1)}=\frac{x-2}{x-1}$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 3

(Exercise 18.3, Review and Consolidation, Problem 3) Calculate:

(1) $\frac{2 a}{a^2-4}-\frac{1}{a-2}$;

(2) $\frac{y}{x+y}+\frac{xy}{y^2-x^2}$;

(3) $\frac{1}{x+1}-1+\frac{1}{1-x}$;

(4) $\frac{2 y}{x-y}-\frac{2 x}{y-x}$.

### Solution to Problem 3

(1) $\frac{2a}{(a+2)(a-2)}-\frac{a+2}{(a+2)(a-2)}=\frac{a-2}{(a+2)(a-2)}=\frac{1}{a+2}$.

(2) $\frac{y}{x+y}+\frac{xy}{(y-x)(y+x)}=\frac{y(y-x)+xy}{(x+y)(y-x)}=\frac{y^2}{(x+y)(y-x)}=\frac{y^2}{y^2-x^2}$.

(3) $\frac{1}{x+1}-1-\frac{1}{x-1}=\frac{(x-1)-(x+1)(x-1)-(x+1)}{(x+1)(x-1)}=\frac{x-1-x^2+1-x-1}{x^2-1}=\frac{-x^2-x-1}{x^2-1}$.

(4) $\frac{2y}{x-y}+\frac{2x}{x-y}=\frac{2x+2y}{x-y}$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 4

(Exercise 18.3, Comprehensive Application, Problem 4) Calculate:

(1) $\left(\frac{a}{a-2}-\frac{a}{a+2}\right)\div\frac{a}{a-2}$;

(2) $\left(\frac{3 a}{a-3}-\frac{a}{a+3}\right)\cdot\frac{a^2-9}{a}$.

### Solution to Problem 4

(1) $\frac{a(a+2)-a(a-2)}{(a-2)(a+2)}\cdot\frac{a-2}{a}=\frac{4a}{(a-2)(a+2)}\cdot\frac{a-2}{a}=\frac{4}{a+2}$.

(2) $\frac{3a(a+3)-a(a-3)}{(a-3)(a+3)}\cdot\frac{(a-3)(a+3)}{a}=\frac{3a^2+9a-a^2+3a}{a}=\frac{2a^2+12a}{a}=2a+12$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 5

(Exercise 18.3, Comprehensive Application, Problem 5) First simplify, then evaluate:

(1) $\left(\frac{x^2-y^2}{xy}\right)^2\div(x+y)^2\cdot\left(\frac{x}{x-y}\right)^3$, where $x=2$, $y=1$.

### Solution to Problem 5

$$\frac{(x^2-y^2)^2}{x^2 y^2}\cdot\frac{1}{(x+y)^2}\cdot\frac{x^3}{(x-y)^3}=\frac{(x+y)^2(x-y)^2}{x^2 y^2}\cdot\frac{1}{(x+y)^2}\cdot\frac{x^3}{(x-y)^3}=\frac{x}{y^2(x-y)}.$$

Substituting $x=2,y=1$, we get $\frac{2}{1\cdot1}=2$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 6

(Exercise 18.3, Comprehensive Application, Problem 6) Calculate: $\frac{1}{1\times2}+\frac{1}{2\times3}+\frac{1}{3\times4}+\cdots+\frac{1}{99\times100}$.

### Solution to Problem 6

Using the telescoping decomposition $\frac{1}{n(n+1)}=\frac1n-\frac{1}{n+1}$:

$$\left(1-\frac12\right)+\left(\frac12-\frac13\right)+\cdots+\left(\frac{1}{99}-\frac{1}{100}\right)=1-\frac{1}{100}=\frac{99}{100}.$$

$\blacksquare$

### Problem 7

(Exercise 18.3, Extension and Exploration, Problem 7) Calculate: $\frac{1}{x(x+1)}+\frac{1}{(x+1)(x+2)}+\frac{1}{(x+2)(x+3)}$.

### Solution to Problem 7

Telescoping cancellation:

$$\left(\frac1x-\frac{1}{x+1}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{x+1}-\frac{1}{x+2}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{x+2}-\frac{1}{x+3}\right)=\frac1x-\frac{1}{x+3}=\frac{3}{x(x+3)}.$$

$\blacksquare$

### Problem 8

(Exercise 18.3, Extension and Exploration, Problem 8) A computer manufacturer produces a certain computer at a cost of $x$ yuan per unit. The company decides: if the selling price of each computer is $y$ yuan, what is the profit margin (profit margin $=\frac{售价-成本}{成本}$)? When $x=5000$ and $y=6000$, what is the profit margin?

### Solution to Problem 8

Profit margin $=\frac{y-x}{x}$. Substituting $x=5000,y=6000$, we get $\frac{6000-5000}{5000}=\frac{1000}{5000}=0.2=20\%$. $\blacksquare$

### Reading and Thinking 1

(Reading and Thinking, Can the Water in a Container Be Poured Out?) A container holds $1\ \text{L}$ of water; the $1$-st pour removes $\frac12\ \text{L}$, the $2$-nd pour removes $\frac{1}{2\times3}\ \text{L}$, the $3$-rd pour removes $\frac{1}{3\times4}\ \text{L}$, $\cdots\cdots$ the $n$-th pour removes $\frac{1}{n(n+1)}\ \text{L}$. How much water has been poured out after $n$ pours? Can all the water be poured out?

### Solution to Reading and Thinking 1

The total amount of water poured out after $n$ pours is

$$S_n=\frac12+\frac{1}{2\times3}+\frac{1}{3\times4}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n(n+1)}.$$

Using the telescoping decomposition $\frac{1}{k(k+1)}=\frac1k-\frac{1}{k+1}$, we have

$$S_n=\left(1-\frac12\right)+\left(\frac12-\frac13\right)+\cdots+\left(\frac1n-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)=1-\frac{1}{n+1}=\frac{n}{n+1}.$$

No matter how large $n$ is, $S_n=\frac{n}{n+1}<1$, so the $1\ \text{L}$ of water in the container can never be completely poured out. $\blacksquare$
