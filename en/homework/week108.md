> **Basic Knowledge: Constants and Variables**<br>In a process of change, we call a quantity whose value remains constant a constant, and a quantity whose value changes a variable.
> **Basic Knowledge: Function**<br>Generally, in a process of change, if there are two variables $x$ and $y$, and for each definite value of $x$, $y$ has a unique definite value corresponding to it, then we say $x$ is the independent variable and $y$ is a function of $x$. If when $x=a$ then $y=b$, then $b$ is called the function value when the independent variable equals $a$.
> **Basic Knowledge: Analytic Expression of a Function**<br>Using a mathematical expression in the independent variable, as in $y=50-0.1x$, to express the relation between the function and the independent variable is a common way of representing a function; such an expression is called the analytic expression of the function.

## Exercise 1

(Exercise 22.1, Problem 1) Identify the constants and variables in the following problems:

(1) Water is poured into a pool at a rate of $0.1\ \text{m}^3/\text{min}$. Let the filling time be $x\ \text{min}$ and the amount of water filled be $y\ \text{m}^3$.

(2) The annual gross domestic product (GDP) of China during the 13th Five-Year Plan is shown in the table below.

| Year $x$ | $2016$ | $2017$ | $2018$ | $2019$ | $2020$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GDP $y$/100 million yuan | $746395.1$ | $832035.9$ | $919281.1$ | $986515.2$ | $1013567.0$ |

(3) The base of a parallelogram is $5$, the height $h$ may vary arbitrarily, and the area is $S$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

(1) By **Basic Knowledge: Constants and Variables**, the value of the filling rate $0.1\ \text{m}^3/\text{min}$ remains constant, so the filling rate is a constant. The values of the filling time $x$ and the filled amount $y$ change, so the filling time $x$ and the filled amount $y$ are variables.

(2) By **Basic Knowledge: Constants and Variables**, the values of the year $x$ and the GDP $y$ both change, so the year $x$ and the GDP $y$ are variables; in this problem there is no quantity whose value remains constant.

(3) By **Basic Knowledge: Constants and Variables**, the base length $5$ of the parallelogram remains constant, so the base length is a constant. The values of the height $h$ and the area $S$ change, so the height $h$ and the area $S$ are variables. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 6

(Exercise 22.1, Problem 2) The upper base of a trapezoid is $2\ \text{cm}$, its height is $3\ \text{cm}$, and the lower base $x$ (unit: cm) is greater than the upper base but no more than $5\ \text{cm}$. Write the analytic expression for the trapezoid area $S$ (unit: $\text{cm}^2$) as a function of $x$, and state the range of the independent variable $x$.

### Solution to Exercise 6

By the trapezoid area formula, the area equals half the sum of the two bases times the height. The upper base is $2\ \text{cm}$, the lower base is $x\ \text{cm}$, and the height is $3\ \text{cm}$, so

$$
S = \frac{\left(2+x\right) \times 3}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\left(x+2\right).
$$

By **Basic Knowledge: Analytic Expression of a Function**, $S=\frac{3}{2}\left(x+2\right)$ is the desired analytic expression.

The lower base $x$ is greater than the upper base $2\ \text{cm}$, i.e. $x> 2$; the lower base $x$ is no more than $5\ \text{cm}$, i.e. $x \leq 5$. Hence the range of the independent variable $x$ is $2 < x \leq 5$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 4

(Exercise 22.1, Review and Consolidation, Problem 4) The base of a triangle is $5$, and the area $S$ changes with the height $h$ to that base. Identify the constants and variables, the independent variable and the function, write the analytic expression, and state the range of the independent variable.

### Solution to Problem 4

By **Basic Knowledge: Constants and Variables**, the base length $5$ of the triangle remains constant, so the base length is a constant. The height $h$ to the base and the area $S$ change, so $h$ and $S$ are variables.

By **Basic Knowledge: Function**, for each definite value of the height $h$ to the base, the area $S$ has a unique definite value corresponding to it, so $S$ is a function of $h$, where $h$ is the independent variable and $S$ is a function of $h$.

By the triangle area formula, the area equals half the base times the height, so

$$
S = \frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times h = \frac{5}{2}h.
$$

The height $h$ cannot be negative, so the range of the independent variable $h$ is $h \geq 0$. $\blacksquare$

(1) Over what range of the independent variable $x$ is the analytic expression meaningful?

## Problem 9

(Exercise 22.1, Extension and Exploration, Problem 9) People forget what they have learned; the forgetting curve describes how much of newly learned material the human brain retains. In the figure, the horizontal axis is the time $t$ (unit: days) since first memorization, and the vertical axis is the amount retained $y$ (unit: %).

(1) Is $y$ a function of $t$? Why?

(2) Observing the forgetting curve, can you give advice on reviewing newly learned material?

### Solution to Problem 9
