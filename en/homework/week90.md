> **Definition 18.4.1** (Negative Integer Exponent Power) <br>When $n$ is a positive integer, $a^{-n}=\frac{1}{a^n}$ ($a\ne0$), i.e. $a^{-n}$ is the reciprocal of $a^n$.

> **Definition 18.4.2** (Properties of Integer Exponent Powers) <br>(1) $a^m\cdot a^n=a^{m+n}$; (2) $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$; (3) $(ab)^n=a^n b^n$ ($m,n$ are integers, the base is nonzero).

> **Definition 18.4.3** (Scientific Notation for Positive Numbers Less Than 1) <br>A positive number less than $1$ can be written in the form $a\times10^{-n}$, where $1\le a<10$, and $n$ is a positive integer.

### Exercise 1

(Exercise 18.4, Problem 1) Fill in the blanks:

(1) $3^0=$ \;_\_\; , $3^{-2}=$ \;_\_\; ;

(2) $(-3)^0=$ \;_\_\; , $(-3)^{-2}=$ \;_\_\; ;

(3) $b^0=$ \;_\_\; , $b^{-2}=$ \;_\_\; ($b\ne0$).

### Solution to Exercise 1

By the zero exponent power $a^0=1$ ($a\ne0$) and **Definition 18.4.1**.

(1) $3^0=1$, $3^{-2}=\frac19$.

(2) $(-3)^0=1$, $(-3)^{-2}=\frac19$.

(3) $b^0=1$, $b^{-2}=\frac{1}{b^2}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 2

(Exercise 18.4, Problem 2) Calculate:

(1) $x^2 y^{-3}\cdot(x^{-1} y)^3$;

(2) $(2ab^2 c^{-3})^{-2}\div(a^{-2} b)^3$.

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 18.4.2**.

(1) $x^2 y^{-3}\cdot x^{-3} y^3=x^{-1} y^0=\frac1x$.

(2) $(2ab^2 c^{-3})^{-2}\div(a^{-2} b)^3=\frac{1}{4}a^{-2} b^{-4} c^6\div(a^{-6} b^3)=\frac{1}{4}a^4 b^{-7} c^6=\frac{a^4 c^6}{4b^7}$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 1

(Exercise 18.4, Review and Consolidation, Problem 1) Fill in the blanks:

(1) If $(a-3)^{-2}$ is meaningful, then the range of $a$ is \;_\_\; ;

(2) $1\div a^{-1}=$ \;_\_\; ; $a^2\cdot a^{-2}=$ \;_\_\; ; $(-ab^{-1})^{-2}=$ \;_\_\; .

### Solution to Problem 1

(1) $(a-3)^{-2}=\frac{1}{(a-3)^2}$, requiring $a-3\ne0$, i.e. $a\ne3$.

(2) $1\div a^{-1}=a$; $a^2\cdot a^{-2}=a^0=1$; $(-ab^{-1})^{-2}=\frac{1}{(-a/b)^2}=\frac{b^2}{a^2}$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 3

(Exercise 18.4, Review and Consolidation, Problem 3) Calculate:

(1) $3a^{-2} b\cdot2ab^{-2}$;

(2) $4xy^2 z\div(-2x^{-2} y z^{-1})$;

(3) $(-3ab^{-1})^3$;

(4) $(2m^2 n^{-2})^2\cdot3m^{-3} n^3$.

### Solution to Problem 3

By **Definition 18.4.2**.

(1) $3a^{-2} b\cdot2ab^{-2}=6a^{-1} b^{-1}=\frac{6}{ab}$.

(2) $4xy^2 z\div(-2x^{-2} y z^{-1})=-2x^3 y z^2$.

(3) $(-3ab^{-1})^3=-27a^3 b^{-3}=-\frac{27a^3}{b^3}$.

(4) $4m^4 n^{-4}\cdot3m^{-3} n^3=12m n^{-1}=\frac{12m}{n}$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 6

(Exercise 18.4, Comprehensive Application, Problem 6) Calculate:

(1) $\frac{a^{-2} b^3(-3a^{-1} b^2)}{6a^{-3} b^{-2}}$;

(2) $\left(\frac{x^2+xy}{x}\right)^{-1}\div\left(\frac{x-y}{x}\cdot\frac{x}{y-x}\right)^{-3}$.

### Solution to Problem 6

(1) $\frac{-3a^{-3} b^5}{6a^{-3} b^{-2}}=-\frac12 b^7$.

(2) $\left(\frac{x(x+y)}{x}\right)^{-1}\div\left(\frac{x-y}{x}\cdot\frac{x}{-(x-y)}\right)^{-3}=\frac{1}{x+y}\div(-1)^{-3}=\frac{1}{x+y}\div(-1)=-\frac{1}{x+y}$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 7

(Exercise 18.4, Comprehensive Application, Problem 7) Given $x+x^{-1}=3$, find the values of $x^2+x^{-2}$ and $x^4+x^{-4}$.

### Solution to Problem 7

$(x+x^{-1})^2=x^2+2+x^{-2}=9$, hence $x^2+x^{-2}=7$.

$(x^2+x^{-2})^2=x^4+2+x^{-4}=49$, hence $x^4+x^{-4}=47$. $\blacksquare$

### Problem 8

(Exercise 18.4, Extension and Exploration, Problem 8) Given that the mass of $1$ water molecule is approximately $3\times10^{-26}\ \text{kg}$, and $1$ drop of water (taking $20$ drops as $1\ \text{g}$) contains approximately how many water molecules?

### Solution to Problem 8

The mass of $1$ drop of water $=\frac{1}{20}\ \text{g}=5\times10^{-5}\ \text{kg}$.

The number of water molecules $=\frac{5\times10^{-5}}{3\times10^{-26}}=\frac{5}{3}\times10^{21}\approx1.67\times10^{21}$ molecules. $\blacksquare$
