> **Basic Knowledge: Inverse Proportional Function**<br>A function of the form $y=\frac{k}{x}$ (where $k$ is a constant and $k\ne0$) is called an inverse proportional function, and the range of the independent variable $x$ is all real numbers except $0$.
> **Basic Knowledge: Graph and Properties of the Inverse Proportional Function**<br>The graph of y = k/x is a hyperbola. When k > 0, branches are in quadrants I and III; when k < 0, in quadrants II and IV.

> **Basic Knowledge: Graph and Properties of the Inverse Proportional Function** <br>The graph of $y=\frac{k}{x}$ is a hyperbola. When $k> 0$, the two branches lie in the first and third quadrants, and in each quadrant $y$ decreases as $x$ increases; when $k< 0$, the two branches lie in the second and fourth quadrants, and in each quadrant $y$ increases as $x$ increases.

## Review Problem 1

(Review Problem 26, Review and Consolidation, Problem 1) Express the following functions with their expressions:

(1) The area of a triangle is $12\ \text{cm}^2$, and one side $a$ (unit: $\text{cm}$) is a function of the height $h$ (unit: $\text{cm}$) to this side;

(2) The volume of a cone is $50\ \text{cm}^3$, and its height $h$ (unit: $\text{cm}$) is a function of the base area $S$ (unit: $\text{cm}^2$).

### Solution to Review Problem 1

(1) The area of the triangle is $S=\frac{1}{2}ah=12$, so $ah=24$ and $a=\frac{24}{h}$.

(2) The volume of the cone is $V=\frac{1}{3}Sh=50$, so $Sh=150$ and $h=\frac{150}{S}$. $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 2

(Review Problem 26, Review and Consolidation, Problem 2) Fill in the blanks:

For the function $y=\frac{3}{x}$, when $x> 0$, $y\;\_\_\_\;0$, and at this time the graph of the function lies in the $\;\_\_\_\;$ quadrant; for the function $y=-\frac{3}{x}$, when $x< 0$, $y\;\_\_\_\;0$, and at this time the graph of the function lies in the $\;\_\_\_\;$ quadrant.

### Solution to Review Problem 2

By **Basic Knowledge: Graph and Properties of the Inverse Proportional Function**.

$y=\frac{3}{x}$, $k=3> 0$; when $x> 0$, $y> 0$, and the graph lies in the first quadrant.

$y=-\frac{3}{x}$, $k=-3< 0$; when $x< 0$, $y=\frac{-3}{x}=\frac{-3}{\text{负数}}> 0$, and the graph lies in the first quadrant. $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 4

(Review Problem 26, Review and Consolidation, Problem 4) Among the following four relations, the one in which $y$ is an inverse proportional function of $x$ is ( ).

(A) $y=\frac{1}{x^2}$; (B) $yx=-\sqrt{3}$; (C) $y=5x+6$; (D) $\sqrt{x}=\frac{1}{y}$.

### Solution to Review Problem 4

By **Basic Knowledge**, an inverse proportional function has the form $y=\frac{k}{x}$.

(A) $y=\frac{1}{x^2}$, $y$ is inversely proportional to $x^2$; (B) $yx=-\sqrt{3}$ means $y=\frac{-\sqrt{3}}{x}$, which is an inverse proportional function; (C) is a linear function; (D) $y=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$, which is not. So choose (B). $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 6

(Review Problem 26, Comprehensive Application, Problem 6) The areas of the three faces $A,B,C$ of a brick are in the ratio $4:2:1$. If the $B$ face is placed downward on the ground, the pressure on the ground is $a\ \text{Pa}$. What are the pressures on the ground when the $A$ face and the $C$ face are placed downward, respectively?

### Solution to Review Problem 6

The gravity $F$ of the brick is constant; by $p=\frac{F}{S}$, the pressure is inversely proportional to the contact area.

Let the areas of the $A,B,C$ faces be $4S,2S,S$, respectively. When the $B$ face is down, $p_B=\frac{F}{2S}=a$.

When the $A$ face is down, $p_A=\frac{F}{4S}=\frac{a}{2}$.

When the $C$ face is down, $p_C=\frac{F}{S}=2a$. $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 7

(Review Problem 26, Comprehensive Application, Problem 7) It is known that the service life of a certain brand of display is about $2\times10^4\ \text{h}$.

(1) What is the functional relationship between the number of days $d$ this display can work and the average number of hours $t$ worked per day?

(2) If it works $10\ \text{h}$ per day on average, about how long can this display be used?

### Solution to Review Problem 7

(1) The total life equals the number of days times the daily hours, i.e. $dt=2\times10^4$, so $d=\frac{20000}{t}$.

(2) When $t=10$, $d=\frac{20000}{10}=2000$ days. $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 8

(Review Problem 26, Comprehensive Application, Problem 8) Match the expressions of the following functions with their graphs:

(1) $y=\frac{2}{x}$; (2) $y=\frac{2}{\left|x\right|}$; (3) $y=-\frac{2}{x}$; (4) $y=-\frac{2}{\left|x\right|}$.

Graph (A): both branches lie above the $x$-axis; Graph (B): both branches lie in the first and third quadrants; Graph (C): both branches lie in the second and fourth quadrants; Graph (D): both branches lie below the $x$-axis.

### Solution to Review Problem 8

By **Basic Knowledge**.

(1) $y=\frac{2}{x}$, $k=2> 0$, lies in the first and third quadrants, corresponding to (B).

(2) $y=\frac{2}{\left|x\right|}$, whether $x$ is positive or negative, $y> 0$, both branches lie above the $x$-axis, corresponding to (A).

(3) $y=-\frac{2}{x}$, $k=-2< 0$, lies in the second and fourth quadrants, corresponding to (C).

(4) $y=-\frac{2}{\left|x\right|}$, whether $x$ is positive or negative, $y< 0$, both branches lie below the $x$-axis, corresponding to (D).

Thus (1)-(B), (2)-(A), (3)-(C), (4)-(D). $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 9

(Review Problem 26, Extension and Exploration, Problem 9) Can the graphs of two different inverse proportional functions intersect? Why?

### Solution to Review Problem 9

Let the two different inverse proportional functions be $y=\frac{k_1}{x}$ and $y=\frac{k_2}{x}$, with $k_1\ne k_2$.

If they intersect, then $\frac{k_1}{x}=\frac{k_2}{x}$, i.e. $k_1=k_2$, which contradicts $k_1\ne k_2$.

Therefore the graphs of two different inverse proportional functions cannot intersect. $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 10

(Review Problem 26, Extension and Exploration, Problem 10) In the same rectangular coordinate system, if the graph of the direct proportional function $y=k_1x$ and the graph of the inverse proportional function $y=\frac{k_2}{x}$ have no intersection point, determine the range of $k_1k_2$.

### Solution to Review Problem 10

If there is an intersection point, then $k_1x=\frac{k_2}{x}$, i.e. $k_1x^2=k_2$.

When $k_1k_2> 0$, $x^2=\frac{k_2}{k_1}> 0$, which has a solution, and the two graphs intersect.

When $k_1k_2< 0$, $x^2=\frac{k_2}{k_1}< 0$, which has no solution, and the two graphs have no intersection point.

Therefore $k_1k_2< 0$. $\blacksquare$

## Review Problem 11

(Review Problem 26, Extension and Exploration, Problem 11) The city government plans to build a water conservancy project. The total volume of earth and stone to be transported for the project is $10^6\ \text{m}^3$, and a certain transport company undertakes the task of transporting the earth and stone.

(1) What is the functional relationship between the company's average transport speed $v$ (unit: $\text{m}^3/$day) and the time $t$ (unit: days) required to complete the transport task?

(2) The transport company has a total of $100$ trucks, and can transport $10^4\ \text{m}^3$ of earth and stone per day. How long does the company need to complete all the transport tasks?

(3) After the company has worked for $40$ days at the speed in problem (2), due to the needs of the project schedule, all the remaining transport tasks must be completed within $50$ days. At least how many additional trucks should the company add?

### Solution to Review Problem 11

(1) From $vt=10^6$, we get $v=\frac{10^6}{t}$.

(2) When $v=10^4$, $t=\frac{10^6}{10^4}=100$ days.

(3) In $40$ days, $40\times10^4=4\times10^5\ \text{m}^3$ has been transported, leaving $6\times10^5\ \text{m}^3$. To complete within $50$ days requires transporting $\frac{6\times10^5}{50}=1.2\times10^4\ \text{m}^3$ per day. Each truck transports $\frac{10^4}{100}=100\ \text{m}^3$ per day, so $\frac{1.2\times10^4}{100}=120$ trucks are needed; there are already $100$ trucks, so at least $20$ more should be added. $\blacksquare$
