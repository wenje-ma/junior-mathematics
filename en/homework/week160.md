> **Basic Knowledge: Inverse Proportional Function**<br>A function of the form $y=\frac{k}{x}$ (where $k$ is a constant and $k\ne0$) is called an inverse proportional function, and the range of the independent variable $x$ is all real numbers except $0$.
> **Basic Knowledge: Principle of the Lever**<br>Resistance * resistance arm = effort * effort arm: F1*l1 = F2*l2.

---


> **Basic Knowledge: Graph and Properties of the Inverse Proportional Function** <br>The graph of $y=\frac{k}{x}$ is a hyperbola. When $k> 0$, the two branches lie in the first and third quadrants, and in each quadrant $y$ decreases as $x$ increases; when $k< 0$, the two branches lie in the second and fourth quadrants, and in each quadrant $y$ increases as $x$ increases.

> **Basic Knowledge: Principle of the Lever** <br>the resistance $\times$ the resistance arm $=$ the effort $\times$ the effort arm, i.e. $F_1l_1=F_2l_2$.

### Activity 1

(Math Activity, Activity 1) The following table gives the length and width of $10$ rectangles of equal area; please complete the table. Let $\angle A$ be the common angle of these $10$ rectangles, draw the $10$ rectangles, then take the $10$ opposite vertices of $\angle A$, and connect the $10$ points in order with a smooth curve. Is this curve a branch of the graph of an inverse proportional function? Why?

Length/cm: $1,2,3,4,5,\;\_\_,\;\_\_,\;\_\_,\;\_\_,\;\_\_$

Width/cm: $\_\_,\_\_,\_\_,\_\_,2,\frac{5}{3},\frac{10}{7},\frac{5}{4},\frac{10}{9},1$

The figure shows 10 rectangles of area $10\ \text{cm}^2$ sharing a common angle $\angle A$ at the origin (one side along the $x$-axis, the other along the $y$-axis), their opposite (upper-right) vertices being $\left(1,10\right)$, $\left(2,5\right)$, $\left(3,\frac{10}{3}\right)$, $\left(4,\frac{5}{2}\right)$, $\left(5,2\right)$, $\left(6,\frac{5}{3}\right)$, $\left(7,\frac{10}{7}\right)$, $\left(8,\frac{5}{4}\right)$, $\left(9,\frac{10}{9}\right)$, and $\left(10,1\right)$, connected in order by a smooth curve.

### Solution to Activity 1

From width $=2$ and length $=5$, each rectangle has area $10$, so length $\times$ width $=10$, i.e. width $=\frac{10}{\text{长}}$.

When the length is $1$, width $=10$; when it is $2$, width $=5$; when it is $3$, width $=\frac{10}{3}$; when it is $4$, width $=\frac{5}{2}$; when it is $5$, width $=2$.

When the width is $\frac{5}{3}$, length $=6$; when it is $\frac{10}{7}$, length $=7$; when it is $\frac{5}{4}$, length $=8$; when it is $\frac{10}{9}$, length $=9$; when it is $1$, length $=10$.

Thus the lengths are filled in successively as $6,7,8,9,10$; the widths as $10,5,\frac{10}{3},\frac{5}{2}$.

The coordinates of the opposite vertices are $\left(\text{长},\text{宽}\right)$, satisfying length $\times$ width $=10$, i.e. $y=\frac{10}{x}$; this is the branch of the inverse proportional function in the first quadrant when $k=10> 0$. $\blacksquare$

### Activity 2

(Math Activity, Activity 2) Take a uniform wooden rod of length $100\ \text{cm}$, tie a string to its midpoint $O$ and suspend it. On the left side of the midpoint $O$, at a distance of $25\ \text{cm}$ from $O$, hang an object weighing $9.8\ \text{N}$; on the right side of $O$, pull downward with a spring scale so that the rod is horizontal. Vary the distance $L$ (unit: $\text{cm}$) between the spring scale and the midpoint $O$, observe how the reading $F$ (unit: $\text{N}$) of the spring scale changes, and fill in the following table: $L=5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40,45$. Plot the points with $L$ as the x-coordinate and $F$ as the y-coordinate, and connect them. Is this curve a branch of the graph of an inverse proportional function? Is the point $\left(50,4.9\right)$ on this curve?

The figure shows a uniform wooden rod suspended horizontally by a string at its midpoint $O$; on the left side, at $25\ \text{cm}$ from $O$, hangs an object weighing $9.8\ \text{N}$, and on the right side, at distance $L$ from $O$, a spring scale pulls downward vertically to keep the rod in horizontal balance.

### Solution to Activity 2

By **Basic Knowledge: Principle of the Lever**, the moment on the left is $9.8\times25=245$; at balance, $F\cdot L=245$, i.e. $F=\frac{245}{L}$.

$L=5\Rightarrow F=49$; $10\Rightarrow24.5$; $15\Rightarrow\frac{49}{3}\approx16.33$; $20\Rightarrow12.25$; $25\Rightarrow9.8$; $30\Rightarrow\frac{49}{6}\approx8.17$; $35\Rightarrow7$; $40\Rightarrow6.125$; $45\Rightarrow\frac{49}{9}\approx5.44$.

$F=\frac{245}{L}$ is an inverse proportional function, $k=245> 0$, and it is the branch in the first quadrant.

For the point $\left(50,4.9\right)$: $F=\frac{245}{50}=4.9$, which satisfies the expression, so the point is on the curve. $\blacksquare$
