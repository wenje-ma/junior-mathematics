> **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**<br>The result obtained by substituting numerical values for the letters in an algebraic expression and computing according to the operations in the expression is called the value of the algebraic expression. When the letters take different numerical values, the value of the algebraic expression generally also differs.

> **Basic Knowledge: Forming Algebraic Expressions**<br>An algebraic expression is formed by connecting numbers or letters representing numbers with operation symbols.

## Exercise 1

(Exercise 3.2, Problem 1) Fill in the diagram: input $a$, compute output according to $2-3a$. When $a$ takes respectively $10$, $4$, $0$, $-3$, $2$, what are the outputs?

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**, substitute each value of $a$ into $2-3a$.

When $a=10$, $2-3\times 10=-28$; when $a=4$, $2-3\times 4=-10$; when $a=0$, $2-0=2$; when $a=-3$, $2-3\times\left(-3\right)=11$; when $a=2$, $2-3\times 2=-4$.

Hence the outputs in turn are $-28$, $-10$, $2$, $11$, $-4$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Exercise 3.2, Problem 2) According to the following values of $x$ and $y$, find respectively the value of the algebraic expression $x^{2}+2xy+y^{2}$:

(1) $x=2$, $y=-3$;

(2) $x=\frac{1}{2}$, $y=-4$.

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**.

(1) When $x=2$, $y=-3$, $x^{2}+2xy+y^{2}=4+2\times 2\times\left(-3\right)+9=4-12+9=1$.

(2) When $x=\frac{1}{2}$, $y=-4$, $x^{2}+2xy+y^{2}=\frac{1}{4}+2\times\frac{1}{2}\times\left(-4\right)+16=\frac{1}{4}-4+16=\frac{49}{4}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Exercise 3.2, Problem 3) A car departs from Location A, travels $3.5\ \text{km}$, and then travels at a speed of $v\ \text{km/h}$ for $t\ \text{h}$. Find the total distance traveled by the car. If $v=56$ and $t=0.5$, find the total distance traveled by the car.

### Solution to Exercise 3

By **Basic Knowledge: Forming Algebraic Expressions**, the total distance is $\left(3.5+vt\right)\ \text{km}$.

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**, when $v=56$ and $t=0.5$,

$$3.5+vt=3.5+56\times 0.5=3.5+28=31.5.$$

Hence the total distance traveled by the car is $31.5\ \text{km}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Exercise 3.2, Problem 1) Fill in the blanks.

(1) If $a$ and $b$ respectively denote the base and height of a parallelogram, then the area $S=\;\_\_\;$; when $a=2\ \text{cm}$ and $b=3\ \text{cm}$, $S=\;\_\_\;\text{cm}^{2}$.

(2) If $a$ and $b$ respectively denote the upper and lower bases of a trapezoid, and $h$ denotes the height of the trapezoid, then the area $S=\;\_\_\;$; when $a=2\ \text{cm}$, $b=4\ \text{cm}$, and $h=5\ \text{cm}$, $S=\;\_\_\;\text{cm}^{2}$.

### Solution to Exercise 4

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**.

(1) The area of the parallelogram is $S=ab$; when $a=2$ and $b=3$, $S=2\times 3=6\ \text{cm}^{2}$.

(2) The area of the trapezoid is $S=\frac{\left(a+b\right)h}{2}$; when $a=2$, $b=4$, and $h=5$, $S=\frac{\left(2+4\right)\times 5}{2}=15\ \text{cm}^{2}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Exercise 3.2, Problem 2) The length of a rectangular cardboard box is $a$, and both its width and height are $b$. Express the volume $V$ of this box in an algebraic expression. When $a=60\ \text{cm}$ and $b=40\ \text{cm}$, find the volume of this box.

### Solution to Exercise 5

By **Basic Knowledge: Forming Algebraic Expressions**, the volume of the rectangular prism is $V=ab^{2}$.

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**, when $a=60$ and $b=40$,

$$V=60\times 40^{2}=60\times 1600=96000.$$

Hence the volume of this cardboard box is $96000\ \text{cm}^{3}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 6

(Exercise 3.2, Problem 3) As shown in the figure, express the area of the annulus in an algebraic expression. When $R=15\ \text{cm}$ and $r=10\ \text{cm}$, find the area of the annulus (take $\pi$ as $3.14$).

### Solution to Exercise 6

By **Basic Knowledge: Forming Algebraic Expressions**, the area of the annulus is $S=\pi R^{2}-\pi r^{2}=\pi\left(R^{2}-r^{2}\right)$.

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**, when $R=15$ and $r=10$,

$$S=3.14\times\left(15^{2}-10^{2}\right)=3.14\times 125=392.5.$$

Hence the area of the annulus is $392.5\ \text{cm}^{2}$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 2

(Exercise 3.2, Review and Consolidation, Problem 2) Given $a=12$ and $b=-18$, find the values of the algebraic expressions $a+b$, $a-b$, $ab$, and $\frac{b}{a}$.

### Solution to Problem 2

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**.

$$a+b=12+\left(-18\right)=-6;\quad a-b=12-\left(-18\right)=30;$$

$$ab=12\times\left(-18\right)=-216;\quad \frac{b}{a}=\frac{-18}{12}=-\frac{3}{2}.$$

$\blacksquare$

## Problem 3

(Exercise 3.2, Review and Consolidation, Problem 3) According to the following values of $a$ and $b$, find respectively the values of the algebraic expressions $a^{2}+b^{2}$ and $\left(a+b\right)^{2}$:

(1) $a=3$, $b=-2$;

(2) $a=-3$, $b=2$.

### Solution to Problem 3

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**.

(1) When $a=3$ and $b=-2$, $a^{2}+b^{2}=9+4=13$, and $\left(a+b\right)^{2}=\left(3-2\right)^{2}=1$.

(2) When $a=-3$ and $b=2$, $a^{2}+b^{2}=9+4=13$, and $\left(a+b\right)^{2}=\left(-3+2\right)^{2}=1$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 4

(Exercise 3.2, Review and Consolidation, Problem 4) Find the values of the following algebraic expressions:

(1) $\frac{n^{2}+1}{n-1}$, where $n=4$;

(2) $\left(a-c\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{4}b$, where $a=7$, $b=3$, $c=5$.

### Solution to Problem 4

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**.

(2) When $a=7$, $b=3$, and $c=5$, $\left(a-c\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{4}b=\left(7-5\right)^{2}+\frac{3}{4}=4+\frac{3}{4}=\frac{19}{4}$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 5

(Exercise 3.2, Review and Consolidation, Problem 5) The volume of a cone is known to be $V=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}h$, where $r$ is the base radius and $h$ is the height of the cone. When $r=15\ \text{cm}$ and $h=16\ \text{cm}$, find the volume of the cone (take $\pi$ as $3.14$).

### Solution to Problem 5

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**.

$$V=\frac{1}{3}\times 3.14\times 15^{2}\times 16=\frac{1}{3}\times 3.14\times 225\times 16=3768.$$

Hence the volume of the cone is $3768\ \text{cm}^{3}$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 6

(Exercise 3.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 6) A section of steel pipe has the shape of an annular cylinder. If the radius of the large circle is $R$, the radius of the small circle is $r$, and the length of the steel pipe is $a$, express the volume $V$ of this section of steel pipe in an algebraic expression. When $R=30\ \text{mm}$, $r=15\ \text{mm}$, and $a=120\ \text{mm}$, find the volume of this section of steel pipe (take $\pi$ as $3.14$).

### Solution to Problem 6

By **Basic Knowledge: Forming Algebraic Expressions**, the volume of the steel pipe equals the base area (annulus) times the length, $V=\pi\left(R^{2}-r^{2}\right)a$.

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**, when $R=30$, $r=15$, and $a=120$,

$$V=3.14\times\left(30^{2}-15^{2}\right)\times 120=3.14\times 675\times 120=254340.$$

Hence the volume of this section of steel pipe is $254340\ \text{mm}^{3}$. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 7

(Exercise 3.2, Comprehensive Application, Problem 7) $A$ and $B$ are $s\ \text{km}$ apart. Two people, Jia and Yi, drive from $A$ to $B$ at speeds of $a\ \text{km/h}$ and $b\ \text{km/h}$ respectively, and Jia takes less time.

(1) Express in an algebraic expression how much less time Jia takes than Yi;

(2) When $s=180$, $a=72$, and $b=60$, find the value of the algebraic expression in (1), and explain the practical meaning of this value.

### Solution to Problem 7

By **Basic Knowledge: Forming Algebraic Expressions**, time $=$ distance $\div$ speed.

(1) Jia takes $\frac{s}{a}\ \text{h}$, Yi takes $\frac{s}{b}\ \text{h}$, and Jia takes $\left(\frac{s}{b}-\frac{s}{a}\right)\ \text{h}$ less than Yi.

(2) By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**, when $s=180$, $a=72$, and $b=60$,

$$\frac{s}{b}-\frac{s}{a}=\frac{180}{60}-\frac{180}{72}=3-2.5=0.5.$$

Hence Jia takes $0.5\ \text{h}$ less than Yi, that is, Jia arrives half an hour earlier than Yi. $\blacksquare$

## Problem 8

(Exercise 3.2, Extension and Exploration, Problem 8) The conversion relationship between the Celsius scale $C$ and the Fahrenheit scale $F$ is $C=\frac{5}{9}\left(F-32\right)$. (1) Convert $25\,^\circ\text{C}$ to degrees Fahrenheit; (2) convert $-4\,^\circ\text{F}$ to degrees Celsius.

### Solution to Problem 8

By **Basic Knowledge: Value of an Algebraic Expression**.

(1) Inverting $C=\frac{5}{9}\left(F-32\right)$ gives $F=\frac{9}{5}C+32$. When $C=25$, $F=\frac{9}{5}\times 25+32=45+32=77$, that is, $77\,^\circ\text{F}$.

(2) When $F=-4$, $C=\frac{5}{9}\left(-4-32\right)=\frac{5}{9}\times\left(-36\right)=-20$, that is, $-20\,^\circ\text{C}$. $\blacksquare$
